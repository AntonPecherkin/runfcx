Notes

Where we differ from the official docs

  • App keys. We use gasless keys (KEY_ADD). Most guides still show the onchain key registry.
  • Writes. We send through our own read node. It relays to validators.
  • Ports. We keep 3381 and 3383 private and use an SSH tunnel.
  • Disk. Official docs say 1.5 TB in one place and 2 TB in another. Our measured peak: [peak disk].
