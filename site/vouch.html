Vouch for fcx

The bounty asks for someone else to go through the guide, get a read node and a client working, and then post on Farcaster publicly vouching for the quality of the work. Every extra vouch helps.

Before you post

  • Your node shows ready in fcx status.
  • fcx whoami shows your account, so your client works.

Post it

Write it in your own words: did it work, how long it took, what was hard. Honest beats polished.

From your new node (the strongest proof):

fcx cast "Set up my own Farcaster node and client with fcx: antonpecherkin.github.io/fcx-cli. Took me __ hours. ___"

Or from the Farcaster app, and tag @aap there so we see it.
