What we found running it

  • Half the disk

    Upstream needs2 TB

    fcx needs1 TB

    Upstream keeps every download and a full archive until the end, about 1600 GB at the peak. fcx unpacks as it downloads. Our node ended at 557 GB.

  • Survives a crash

    Upstreamweeks

    fcxresumes

    If the restore stops halfway, upstream rebuilds the missing shards block by block (snapchain#1044). fcx picks up where it left off.
